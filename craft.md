How to build, and how to study what you built.

## Building

1. [Worse Is Better](https://www.dreamsongs.com/WorseIsBetter.html) (Gabriel, 1989 onward)
   - *Why*: The essay, the retraction, and the retraction of the retraction, all on one
     page. Simplicity of implementation beats completeness of design in what survives.
     Every "steel thread first" decision descends from this.

2. [The Site Reliability Workbook](https://sre.google/workbook/table-of-contents/) (Beyer, Murphy, Rensin, Kawahara and Thorne, 2018)
   - *Why*: The practical companion to the SRE book, free online. Service levels, error
     budgets, and on-call as an engineering problem. Read the chapters on SLOs and on
     eliminating toil before writing a runbook.

## Studying

3. [Using thematic analysis in psychology](https://doi.org/10.1191/1478088706qp063oa) (Braun and Clarke, 2006)
   - *Why*: The six-phase method for turning interview transcripts into themes without
     pretending the themes were waiting there to be found. The method behind any
     qualitative study of how practitioners actually work.
